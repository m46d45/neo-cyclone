Please find enclosed our Original Software Publication manuscript describing **Neo-CYCLONE**, an open web studio for **AI-Assisted Construction Operation Simulation** in the tradition of Daniel W. Halpin’s CYCLONE / MicroCYCLONE.

**Why Software Impacts.** Neo-CYCLONE is reusable educational research software rather than a single-case empirical study. The short OSP format fits a clear software description, impact overview, and citable open distribution.

**Software availability.**  
- Source: https://github.com/m46d45/neo-cyclone (MIT License)  
- Archive: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21864969 (v1.6.2.1)  
- Live studio: https://neo-cyclone.vercel.app/  
- Manual: https://neo-cyclone.vercel.app/manual  

**Impact focus.** The primary contribution is pedagogical access: prompt-first Halpin-style modeling, discrete-event metrics (productivity, idleness/waste, unit cost, sensitivity), and a studio-bound AI Assistant that proposes edits without replacing the deterministic simulator. Classroom use in construction-operations teaching at Institut Teknologi Bandung is planned from 24 August 2026.

**Compliance.** Code is publicly available under MIT; metadata follow the OSP template (C1–C9). No competing interests.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and that all authors approve submission.
